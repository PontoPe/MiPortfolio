Hello! My name is Milena. I'm based in Curitiba, Brazil, and am a designer by profession, and a fiction writer by heart. I mention this because I believe they serve a similar purpose, making complicated things land without making them smaller than they are. I apply this principle in my design work with a team at Brazil's largest pediatric hospital, and on my own with Mosaico — an undergraduate thesis growing into an AI writing companion for young learners. From very early on — as early as my winning project “Dear Stars” at Apple's WWDC21 Swift Student Challenge — I've known the intersection of learning, design and problem solving is where all of this is headed!
